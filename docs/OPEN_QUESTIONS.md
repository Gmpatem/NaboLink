# Open decisions / research list

## Before contract freeze
- Final service delivery topology: wired last mile, point-to-multipoint wireless, or both; physical feasibility later.
- Residential access: PPPoE at customer CPE, centralized hotspot, or managed router model.
- Internet-package speeds and fair-usage rules; never guarantee an unsupported upstream rate.
- When does a subscription begin: at successful network commissioning or another approved trigger?
- Exact payment receipt authority for MTN MoMo and Orange Money, cash approvals and refund policy.
- Grace period and bounded offline behavior (network safety vs customer continuity).
- Local server, UPS, site access and recovery plan.

## Before commercial launch
- Upstream plan permits the precise intended resale/service model.
- Cameroon regulator permissions and business registration appropriate to topology.
- Actual gateway and installation hardware validated.
- Merchant account and provider integration approved.
